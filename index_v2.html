Why JR Labs

Supplements
shouldn't require
blind trust.

JR Labs was created around a simple idea: customers should understand what they are consuming, and why it is there.

Most people take supplements on trust alone — trusting a label, a brand or an advert. We think that is the wrong starting point. Our approach is to be clear about the ingredient, clear about the reasoning, and clear about what a product can and cannot do.

01 —

The JR Labs Standard — JQLS

Trust should
be built.
Not claimed.

The JR Labs Quality Standard (JQLS) is the internal framework we are developing to govern how our products are sourced, documented, verified and released.

It is a commitment to how JR Labs intends to operate — supplier approval, ingredient documentation, batch traceability, appropriate verification and controlled release. As our first product moves through development, we will publish what has been completed rather than what is planned.

  1. 01

    Ingredient

    Defined specification for the raw material before anything else begins.

  2. 02

    Supplier

    Manufacturing and supply partners assessed against our quality criteria.

  3. 03

    Documentation

    Specifications and supporting records requested, reviewed and retained.

  4. 04

    Verification

    Appropriate testing and verification for the product and its ingredient.

  5. 05

    Batch

    Traceability from raw-material lot through to the finished batch.

  6. 06

    JR Labs

    Controlled release — a batch reaches customers only once records are complete.

Transparency Evidence Traceability Consistency Accountability

JQLS describes the standard JR Labs is building and implementing. It does not represent completed testing or manufacturing for a product that has not yet been produced.

Our Position

Evidence before hype.

We believe supplement brands should earn trust through clarity, evidence and transparency — not exaggerated promises.

The industry can say

  • More.
  • Faster.
  • Stronger.
  • Revolutionary.

JR Labs asks

  • What's in it?
  • Why is it there?
  • What supports the claim?
  • Can we evidence it?

If we can't evidence it,
we don't say it.

Clear language is not a limitation. It is the point. Where a claim requires regulatory confirmation, we will confirm it before we publish it — and where evidence is limited, we will say so rather than fill the space with marketing.

Our Story

An emerging
UK sports
nutrition
company.

JR Labs was founded out of frustration with unnecessary complexity, marketing hype and uncertainty around supplements.

Our ambition is not to reinvent basic ingredients. Creatine monohydrate does not need reinventing — it needs to be made well, described honestly and supported properly.

What we intend to change is confidence: giving customers a clearer understanding of what they choose to consume, so that a decision to take a supplement is an informed one rather than an act of faith.

“After decades of using supplements ourselves, we kept coming back to the same question: how do you really know what you’re taking?”— Founder perspective
