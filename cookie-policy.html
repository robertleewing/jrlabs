Cookie Policy.

How optional analytics cookies are used on the JR Labs website.

JR Labs Ltd uses optional Google Analytics 4 measurement to understand how visitors use this pre-launch website. Analytics is disabled by default and is enabled only when you choose Accept analytics in the cookie banner.

Essential storage

The website stores your cookie preference in your browser so we can remember whether you accepted or rejected optional analytics. This is required to honour your choice.

Google Analytics

If you accept analytics, the site loads Google Analytics 4 using measurement ID G-3C5567SET1. Google Analytics may set first-party cookies such as _ga and a property-specific _ga_* cookie. We use analytics to understand website visits, page usage, traffic sources and engagement so we can improve the site.

Advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalisation are kept disabled in the JR Labs implementation.

Your choice

You can accept or reject optional analytics when the banner appears. You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings in the footer. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from using the website.

More information

For information about how JR Labs handles personal data, see our Privacy Policy. If you have a question about this policy, please contact JR Labs.

Pre-launch notice

This policy describes the analytics configuration currently implemented on the JR Labs pre-launch website. It should be reviewed again before ecommerce, advertising technology or additional tracking tools are introduced.
