Our Story

Why JR Labs exists.

JR Labs is an emerging UK sports nutrition company. We are at the beginning, and we would rather build carefully than loudly.

“After decades of using supplements ourselves, we kept coming back to the same question: how do you really know what you’re taking?”— Founder perspective

JR Labs began with a feeling most people who buy supplements will recognise: standing in front of a wall of tubs, reading labels that explain very little, and having no reliable way to judge which one deserves your trust.

The industry has become very good at marketing and less consistent at explanation. Ingredients appear without reasoning. Claims outrun evidence. Blends hide quantities. The result is that customers are asked to take products on faith, and faith is a poor basis for something you consume every day.

Supplements should be understandable. Customers should know what they are consuming, and why.

Simple products should not need complicated promises

Our first product is creatine monohydrate — one of the most studied ingredients in sports nutrition. It does not need reinventing. It does not need a new name, an exclusive process or an invented benefit. It needs to be made well, described accurately and supported by proper documentation.

That is the standard we are setting for ourselves: if a product is simple, say so. If a claim requires evidence, provide it. If something is still in development, be honest that it is still in development.

Evidence before marketing

We would rather publish less and be right than publish more and be questioned. Where evidence is strong, we will explain it clearly. Where evidence is limited or emerging, we will say that too. Where wording requires regulatory confirmation, we will confirm it before it appears on our packaging or our website.

This is not caution for its own sake. It is the only way a new brand can genuinely earn confidence rather than borrow it.

Building trust product by product

JR Labs is not trying to launch a large range quickly. Our intention is to build a carefully selected portfolio, beginning with a single product done properly, and to expand only when we can hold each new product to the same standard.

Trust is earned in sequence — the first product, then the second, then the consistency between them. We are at the start of that sequence, and we intend to be judged on it.

Our ambition is not to reinvent basic ingredients. It is to give customers greater confidence in what they choose to consume.

Where we are today

JR Labs Ltd is pre-launch. Our first product — JR Labs 100% Creatine Monohydrate — is in development, and our manufacturing partner is currently being evaluated. This website will be updated as each stage is completed, not before.
