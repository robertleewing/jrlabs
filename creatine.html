The Ingredient

What creatine
monohydrate is.

Creatine is a compound produced naturally in the body and also obtained from foods such as meat and fish. It is stored mainly in skeletal muscle, where it plays a role in the body's short-term energy system.

Creatine monohydrate is the most extensively studied form of supplemental creatine and the form used in the majority of published research. It is a single, well-characterised compound — which is exactly why it suits a first product built around transparency. There is very little to explain, and nothing to hide.

Our product contains creatine monohydrate and nothing else: no flavourings, no fillers, no proprietary blend.
